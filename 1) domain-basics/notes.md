\# Domain Basics – Health Insurance.



\## What problem this industry solves

Health insurance protects individuals from high medical expenses by pooling risk across many members.



\## Key Entities

\- Member: person who receives treatment

\- Provider: hospital or clinic

\- TPA: processes claims

\- Insurer: pays claims



\## Claims Workflow

1\. Admission → policy verified

2\. Treatment → diagnosis + procedure codes assigned

3\. Claim submission

4\. Adjudication → approved/rejected

5\. Payment



\## Important Metrics

\- Loss Ratio = Claims Paid / Premiums

\- Claim Frequency = Claims / Members

\- Average Claim Amount



\## Fraud Patterns

\- Duplicate claims

\- Inception fraud (early claims after policy start)

\- Round number billing

\- Threshold manipulation



\## My Observations

\- Fraud detection is pattern-based, not rule-based

\- Same data can represent both legitimate and fraudulent behavior
